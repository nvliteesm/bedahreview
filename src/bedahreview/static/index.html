BedahReview

Fix it or fight it

Paste your product's reviews. Each one is sorted into what you can fix, what to push back on, what only you can check, and what to keep doing. You get the Bahasa words to act on it.

How to paste 
One review per line. If a review has several lines, put a blank line between reviews.
Stars and variant are optional, in this order:

★★★☆☆  Putih, M   warna tulisannya ijo, pas dateng hitem
4★ bahannya tebel
Pas sampe koyak bajunya

Stars can be ★ symbols, "4★", "4/5" or "bintang 4". A variant needs a comma ("Putih, L") or " | " after it.
Demo only: fetched via a third-party service

shopee.co.id product links only. Complaints first gets the lowest-rated reviews that have comments (3 small runs, about 7 US cents for 10); latest gets the newest ones. Each run costs a few cents, can come back with fewer reviews than you ask for, and sometimes fails. Pasting always works.
