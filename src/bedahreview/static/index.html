How to paste 
One review per line. If a review has several lines, put a blank line between reviews.
Stars and variant are optional, in this order:

★★★☆☆  Putih, M   warna tulisannya ijo, pas dateng hitem
4★ bahannya tebel
Pas sampe koyak bajunya

Stars can be ★ symbols, "4★", "4/5" or "bintang 4". A variant needs a comma ("Putih, L") or " | " after it.
